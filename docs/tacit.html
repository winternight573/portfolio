TACIT — VR Haptics for Emotional Storytelling

An R&D investigation into proactive haptic glove feedback for emotional engagement in VR narrative, culminating in the award-winning experience In The End.

Role Technical Experience Designer, Producer
Team CMU ETC Fall 2025 Team TACIT
Tools Unity 6, Meta SDK, bHaptics TactGlove, Figma, Trello
Links Project site · Docs

01 Overview

Developing a haptics-first design approach to a VR narrative experience through 7 Weeks of rapid prototyping and experimentation to find the best ways to use haptic gloves, followed by 7 weeks of building the final experience where the haptic moment meaningfully impacts the overall player emotion.

How can we minimize audio and visual elements in VR , and let haptic feedback carry the emotional weight of a narrative moment? This was the core question that drove the student pitch team TACIT throughout the semester. We first wanted to challenge the assumption of haptics as a reactive feedback mechanism, and then further push the boundaries of tactile storytelling by creating an experience where the haptic moment would serve as the narrative climax for our intended emotion.

As the producer and technical experience designer, I combined the production timeline with a playtesting-driven design process to lead the team through rapid brainstorming, prototyping and iteration every week, so that the project underwent a sufficiently rigorous investigation of the design space and ultimately delivered a polished final experience within 14 weeks. I led the playtesting strategy and mapped the data analytics back to the project success metric.

Throughout the process, beyond bridging the technical and art teams through project management and documentation methods, I also assisted with asset creation such as Unity prototypes and voiceover recordings, as well as the final documentation and external marketing materials that led to successful conference and art festival submissions such as Laval Virtual Student Competition, Augmented World Expo Art Festival, SIGGRAPH Immersive Pavilion, and Sona Immersive Arts Festival.
